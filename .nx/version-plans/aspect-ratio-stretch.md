A view with an `aspect-ratio` that stretches across its flex line is as wide as its column, or as tall as its row, as in a browser, where the ratio was taken over the stretch. In a column, a height given leaves the ratio nothing to work out; in a row, the height the ratio gives a width in points is the view's least height. An image keeps its picture's proportions.
