On iOS, in a development build (not in Expo Go), `@ng-native/components` draws the iPad menu bar from `<menu-bar>` through UIMenuBuilder, with its items' shortcuts. A `<menu-item>` whose shortcut UIKit's own menus already have (a handled `find` on mod+f, say), or that an earlier item of the app has, goes into the menu bar without the shortcut. A `[shortcut]` whose key iOS can map works from a hardware keyboard on iPhone and iPad, except a chord UIKit's own menus already have, or one a focused text input takes first. `UndoStack` is available on iOS, over one undo manager that every window shares, with each action an undo step of its own, so shake to undo, mod+z and Edit > Undo on an iPad reach the app's actions while no text input, or other view with an undo history of its own, has focus. `[contextMenu]` opens on a long press on iOS, and on a secondary click from an iPad pointer, as a UIContextMenuInteraction on the element's own view, found by its `nativeID` (an element without one is given one), and a `(longPress)` on the same element then does not also fire. On iPadOS, `(fileDrop)` takes files, text and links dragged onto a view, a dropped text file arriving in `files` and not as `text`, and `[dragItem]` lets a view be dragged out, through `DragAndDrop` in `@ng-native/device`.
