On Linux the window's `<split-view>` runs the full height of the window, as on macOS and in libadwaita's own apps: the sidebar has a header bar of its own, and the window's header bar, with its title, back and forward pair, sidebar button and header items, sits over the content pane while the split view is shown. Where the menus are a menu bar (KDE Plasma), the menu bar stays across the top of the window, above both panes.
