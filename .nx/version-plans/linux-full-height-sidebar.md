On Linux the window's `<split-view>` runs the full height of the window in a window with the default title bar, as on macOS and in libadwaita's own apps: the sidebar has a header bar of its own, and the window's header bar, with its title, back and forward pair, sidebar button and header items, sits over the content pane while the split view is shown, and over the whole window while a screen in front covers it. A narrow window that shows the sidebar alone has a Show Content button in the sidebar's header bar. Where the menus are a menu bar (KDE Plasma), the menu bar stays across the top of the window, above both panes. A window whose content runs under its title bar keeps its split view and header bar as they were.
