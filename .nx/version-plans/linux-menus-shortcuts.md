On the GTK desktop host on Linux, the app's menus show as a menu bar under the header bar on KDE Plasma and as the header bar's main menu elsewhere, from Linux's own default bar (no Services, Hide, Minimize or window list); `[shortcut]`, the menus' Ctrl keys, Alt+Left and Alt+Right with mouse buttons 8 and 9 for back and forward, and a view's `[contextMenu]` work, and Return and Escape press a window's `default` and `cancel` `<native-button>` when nothing focused uses them.
