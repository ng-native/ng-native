A view the engine paints on native without a commit, one whose transform or opacity changes again and again, is left to one graph of React Native's animated module a prop. The painter lets the view go before a scroll drive or a scroll-driven animation that sets a prop it paints connects to it, as it already did for a native `@keyframes` animation, where both used to set its transform and the one native updated last was drawn. And a view a graph has moved before, such as a sticky row, has the change that hands it to the painter painted on native, where it used to be left to a commit, which iOS and Android do not apply to a prop a graph has set: a list row taken out of the pool of parked rows kept its parked transform, and was drawn off the screen, blank, at its place in the list.
