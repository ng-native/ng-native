`calc()` of a percentage and a length is supported for a box's size, insets, margins, padding and `translate`, where it was dropped with a warning: `width: calc(100% - 2rem)` and Tailwind's `max-w-[calc(100%-2rem)]`. The engine reads the box the percentage is of back from native and commits the box at what the sum comes to.

A sum of a share of a height and a length, in a height, a least height or a basis down a column, is of the height a column gives the box it is in once that box is as tall as its content, as in a browser, where it was set aside. A sum that moves or sizes a box in such a box makes it taller first.
