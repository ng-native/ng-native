On Linux, `<date-time-picker>` follows GNOME's 12 or 24-hour clock setting, read through the settings portal or GSettings and followed as it changes, with the locale's time format where there is none. Its minute steps by `minuteInterval`, and a `countdown` picker picks hours and minutes on a 24-hour clock. The GTK host also tiles an `<image>` with `resizeMode: 'repeat'` from the top left at the image's size in points, as React Native iOS does.
