The Tailwind `browser` option names the classes that components written for a browser sit under, and a flex container with no direction under one is a row.

With the option set, the whole sheet also reads `::before` and `::after` as boxes of their own,
`visibility`, a clip to nothing, `overflow-x` and `overflow-y`, and `:checked`, `:indeterminate` and
`:enabled` as a browser does.
