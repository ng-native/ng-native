In an app with `provideWebCompat()`, an `<img>` is the platform's image view: it shows its `src` resolved against the page's address, says its `alt`, takes its size from its `width` and `height` or its loaded picture, has `naturalWidth`, `naturalHeight`, `complete`, `load` and `error`, draws an SVG picture, and draws on a canvas once loaded.
