A box written as wide as its content in a right-to-left box, with a physical inset on one side and a logical one on the other, such as `left: 10px; inset-inline-start: 5px`, is fitted between the two as in a browser, where it was stretched between them.

In a right-to-left box `inset-inline-start` is the right, so with `left` it gives the box an inset on each side.
