On iOS 26 and later, a stack page under an opaque `<native-header>` starts below the bar with react-native-screens 4.30 (Expo SDK 58), rather than behind it. From 4.30 react-native-screens lays every screen out under the navigation bar on iOS 26 and later, opaque or not, and insets the content with a safe area view its own stack item wraps it in, which `@ng-native/router` had no equivalent of. The stack outlet now commits each page's content in the same `RNSSafeAreaView` on iOS 26 and later, insetting the top edge while the page's header is shown and not translucent, as react-native-screens does. A translucent header, a large title, a hidden header and a page with no header keep the content under the bar as before. With react-native-screens 4.26 (Expo SDK 57) the view insets by nothing, since the screen already starts below an opaque bar there. Under such a header on iOS 26 and later, `<screen-safe-area-view>` leaves the top edge to the stack, so the bar is not counted twice. Before iOS 26 and on Android nothing changes.
