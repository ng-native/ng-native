`@ng-native/nx` installs in a workspace on Nx 24, which `create-nx-workspace` now creates, as well as on Nx 23. Its peer range on `nx` and its `@nx/devkit` range were `^23.0.0`, so npm refused `nx add @ng-native/nx` in an Nx 24 workspace with `ERESOLVE`. Both now take `^23.0.0 || ^24.0.0`. The `init`, `app`, `library`, `component` and `tailwind` generators and every migration run on Nx 24 as they do on 23.
