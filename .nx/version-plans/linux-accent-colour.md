On Linux below libadwaita 1.6 (RHEL 9 and Ubuntu 24.04 among them), an app takes the desktop's accent colour from the settings portal, as libadwaita 1.6 and later do themselves: the nearest of libadwaita's accent colours, with its text colour darkened on a light window and lightened on a dark one, followed as the accent or the style changes.
