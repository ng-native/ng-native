A `height`, `min-height` or column `flex-basis` that is a percentage of a box as tall as its content is that share of the content's height, as a browser lays out a flex item: `height: 50%` beside a 40px box is 20px, where it was no height at all, and `min-height: 50%` there is 20px, where it was half the screen.
